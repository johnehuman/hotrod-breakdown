# Playoff format and payouts

- Six teams make the playoffs, seeded by record, then points for. Divisions don't affect seeding. No reseeding, no two-week matchups.
- Seeds 1 and 2 get Week 15 byes. Week 15: 3 vs 6 and 4 vs 5. Week 16: 1 vs the 4/5 winner, 2 vs the 3/6 winner. Week 17: championship and third-place game.
- 2026 payouts ($200 buy-in): 1st $1,500, 2nd $500, 3rd $200, most regular-season points $100, high score in Week 13 $50, high score in Week 14 $50.

The Rundown: Week 8

The week's best, worst, luckiest and most avoidable results.

🏆 High Scorer of the Week

Ed — 147.50 points | 11–0 All-Play

Ed won the week's biggest game with the week's biggest score. His 147.50 beat Derrick by 6.94 in the highest-scoring matchup of the season, a combined 288.06 that ranks sixth in the archive. It's his season high, his fourth straight win, and it leaves him alone in first at 7-1. It could have been bigger: Tyler Allgeier's 34.10 sat on his bench. Ed's best possible lineup was 178.50, which would have been the third-highest score in league history.

🍀 Lucky Break

Brett — 103.02 points | 3–8 All-Play

Brett's 103.02 would have lost to eight of the league's other 11 teams. He drew the one score nobody could lose to: Bryan's 39.96. The win moves Brett to 4-4 and keeps him in sixth, where he holds the tiebreaker over Wayne by 35.98 points.

🎲 Not So Lucky

Derrick — 140.56 points | 10–1 All-Play

Derrick outscored 10 teams and still lost. His 140.56 is the highest losing score of 2026, passing Kyle's 132.42 from Week 4, and the third-highest losing score in the archive. It drops him to 5-3 and fifth in the standings while he leads the league in scoring by 80.72 points.

🪑 Bench Blunders

Derrick & Lewis — Two Results, One Swap Each

Derrick started Drake London (1.30) over Malik Washington (12.50). That one swap would have lifted him to 151.76 and a 4.26-point win over Ed, leaving them tied at 6-2.

Lewis started Malik Willis (14.64) at quarterback with Patrick Mahomes (25.44) on his bench. Starting Mahomes would have produced 117.44 and a 3.54-point win over Josh. Instead Lewis falls to 2-6.

🎯 Sharp Shooter

Hannah — 100% Lineup Accuracy

Hannah played her best possible lineup and still lost, 124.88–108.90 to John. The season race changed hands: Josh takes over the accuracy lead at 93.6%, just ahead of Lewis at 93.3%. Lewis led through Week 7; the Mahomes decision cost him the top spot.

Around the League

Ed Stands Alone

Ed is 7-1 and alone in first after beating Derrick. Four managers are now 6-2 or better: Ed, Josh, John and Hannah. In the archive, all 10 teams that reached 6-2 or better after Week 8 made the playoffs. Derrick at 5-3 isn't far behind that line: 11 of 12 teams with his record made it.

Most Points, Fifth Place

Derrick has scored 1,028.10 points, the most through eight weeks in the archive, passing John's 1,012.70 from 2021. His 80.72-point lead over Ed is the largest after Week 8 in league history; the previous high was 41.80 (Lewis, 2022). He still sits fifth, one game behind the 6-2 group and two behind Ed. The points give him one clear advantage: if he finishes tied on record with anyone, he wins the tiebreaker almost every time.

Sixth Place Is a Two-Team Race

Brett and Wayne are both 4-4, with Brett holding sixth on a 35.98-point tiebreaker. History treats 4-4 as an underdog spot: 6 of 16 teams made the playoffs. Wayne has won two straight and gained 11 points of playoff odds this week, the biggest jump in the league. Dawson (3-5) has won two of three and sits one game back.

The Bottom Gets Steeper

Bryan scored 39.96, the lowest eligible score in the archive, breaking his own 49.02 from Week 6. He's lost three straight, and his playoff odds fell from 21% to 7%. No team that was 2-6 after Week 8 has made the playoffs (0 of 7), which leaves Lewis and Kyle needing a first. Kyle has lost six straight. Castano is 0-8; the only other 0-8 team in the archive missed the playoffs.

Week 8 Storylines

The Week 8 Leader Rarely Holds On

In five seasons, the scoring leader after Week 8 won the points title once (Thomas, 2023). Last season Wayne came from 70.10 points back after Week 8. Derrick's lead is nearly double any previous Week 8 lead, and the model still gives him a 76% chance to finish on top, with Ed at 10% and Josh at 9%.

The Champion Climbs Back

Wayne has won two straight, and his playoff odds rose from 33% to 44%, the league's biggest gain this week. The defending champion sits one tiebreaker behind Brett for sixth and plays the 2-6 Lewis next.

The Rookie Keeps Pace

Josh has won three straight, sits 6-2, and trails Ed by only 3.60 points for second in scoring. He also took the season lineup-accuracy lead. Sunday is his first meeting with Ed, with the top seed on the line.

Hannah's Cushion Is Shrinking

Hannah's bye odds fell from 37% to 20% after the loss to John, the week's biggest drop, just ahead of Derrick's. Her remaining schedule includes Derrick once and Josh twice, the hardest path among the 6-2 teams.

How this works

Odds come from 400,000 simulations of weeks 9–14 on the real remaining schedule. Each team's scoring level starts from this season's average, then gets pulled strongly toward the league average. In our 2021–2025 archive, only about a fifth of a team's first-half scoring edge carried into weeks 9–14, so hot starts are expected to cool off. Weekly scores swing by about ±22 points, the league's historical spread.

Seeding uses record, then points-for. Divisions only affect the schedule, not seeding, so ESPN's standings page can show a different order.

The model was tested against all five past seasons, week by week. Its odds for the points leader holding on matched what happened (45% predicted, 43% actual).

Win chances come from this simulation. ESPN projections are the sum of each team's current starters, retrieved Thursday night, and lineups can still change. Odds over time rerun the same model as of each past week. These are estimates, not guarantees. Treat any number as about ±2 points.
